## Why this domain
The product targets the kind of work done by a Rails 8 team "building the Rails 8
platform that powers sales, service, catalog, orders and integrations for a global
manufacturer" (A-SAFE Digital, Software Builder posting, Málaga, R1). Impact Hub models one slice of that
work for an industrial safety-barrier maker: turning a site survey into a justified
barrier specification.

Impact Hub is an independent portfolio project. It does not use any manufacturer's name,
logo, products or data in the product itself (spec constraint C4, change 001).

## What this means for the product
- **The core is a deterministic calculation.** Required energy comes from four facts and
  one formula (R2), so only the rules compute it, exactly and reproducibly (P4).
- **A barrier fits a hazard only if two checks pass.** Its tested energy at a relevant
  angle covers the required energy, and its deflection fits the space behind it (R2, R8).
- **Unknown facts are normal in a survey.** Remote surveys (R4) and moving loads leave
  gaps. A missing fact must show as unknown with a reason, never as 0 (P3).
- **Selection can only move towards more protection.** A wrong low choice injures
  people; a wrong high choice costs money. Same asymmetry as P1.
- **The survey feeds a priced proposal per zone** (R3). That is a later change, as are
  service after installation (R5) and sensor events (R6).
- **Sensor events come next.** Impact sensors (R6) and the posting's interest in
  webhooks and IoT (R1) make a simulated device posting to a webhook the next module
  after the survey (system spec section 4, phase 4).
- **The stack follows the team's own choices** (R9). Change 001 states it in the spec
  as constraint C1 (Inertia + React) and quality requirements Q2 and Q3, which the
  team's interactors and Alba + Typelizer would answer.

## Open questions this research cannot answer
- What PAS 13 itself says about angles, margins and test methods (R2 caveat).
- Which units each market uses: km/h or mph, kg or lb.
- How surveyors record a survey today: paper checklist, spreadsheet or an app.
