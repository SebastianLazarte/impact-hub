## S1. Surveying a warehouse with forklifts (Nadia)
Evidence: assumption

Nadia walks a distribution warehouse with the site's safety manager. At the end of
aisle 4, counterbalance forklifts of 4,000 kg carrying up to 600 kg pass a pedestrian
walkway at up to 8 km/h, and could hit the walkway head-on. Nadia creates the survey
"Northgate DC", adds the hazard point "Aisle 4 walkway" with those facts, an angle of
90° and 600 mm of space behind the line. The page shows a required energy of 11,358 J
and the walkway barriers that cover it. Barriers that cover it but deflect more
than 600 mm are listed as not fitting, with the reason. Nadia adds four more points and
finishes the specification before leaving the site.

## S2. A remote survey with gaps (Nadia)
Evidence: assumption

A customer in another region cannot host a visit, so Nadia guides them by video. They
know the forklift model and speed limit but not the heaviest load it carries. Nadia
records the point with the load as unknown. The page shows the required energy as
unknown because "maximum load not given", and recommends nothing for that point. Nadia
sends the customer one question instead of guessing.

## S3. A hazard no standard barrier covers (Nadia, Tomás)
Evidence: assumption

At a loading bay, a 12,000 kg reach truck with a 3,000 kg load could reach 12 km/h
towards a column at 90°. The required energy, 83,333 J, is above every barrier in the
catalog. The
point is marked "engineering review" and no barrier is recommended. When Tomás reads the
specification, he sees the number and why this point needs an engineer, instead of a
barrier that would not hold.
