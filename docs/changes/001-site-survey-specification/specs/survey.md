# Survey: specification

Status: **Approved** (<date of step 4>)
Pack: `packs/surveys`, uses the impact and catalog packs
Criteria: IH-20, IH-21

## 1. Purpose
Record what a surveyor finds on a site walk, on site or remotely, so the
[impact](impact.md) rules can work on it and a safety manager can check it later.

## 2. Survey (IH-20, IH-21)
A **survey** has a site name, a customer name, a survey date, a mode (`on_site` or
`remote`), optional notes, and one or more hazard points in the order found. Each point
has a name, a zone and the facts of a hazard point ([impact.md](impact.md) section 2),
each fact with its source as the surveyor records it (P3).
A survey and its points are saved together or not at all. While a survey is open, the
values shown for its points follow the current rules of impact.md and the current
catalog (Q1).
